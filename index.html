Welcome to PlantVerse

In this relaxing botanical simulation, you will nurture a unique plant from a tiny seed into a fully grown hybrid, while managing your very own farm, barn, and café.

1. Getting Started 🌱

Open the Botanical Catalog to choose your first seed, and the Adopt a Pet menu for a companion.

2. Nurturing 💧☀️

Use the Water and Sun buttons to keep your plant nourished. Click ⏳ +6h to pass time and grow it through its 5 stages.

3. Pollen & Genetics 🐝🧬

Tap flying bees to collect pollen. Take 3 Pollen to the Genetics Lab (once your plant is grown) to crossbreed new hybrids!

4. Farm & Barn 🚜🐄

In the Farm and Barn tabs, plant crops and raise animals to collect harvestable produce.

5. The Café ☕

Fulfill customer orders in the Café using your harvests to earn coins and rare Superfoods.
